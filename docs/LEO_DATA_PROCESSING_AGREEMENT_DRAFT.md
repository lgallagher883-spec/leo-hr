## 1. Scope and relationship

This Data Processing Agreement forms part of the agreement under which LEO HR LTD supplies the Leo HR service to the Customer. It applies where Leo processes personal data on the Customer's behalf. The Customer is the controller and LEO HR LTD is the processor for that processing, except where either party acts as an independent controller for a separate purpose.

## 2. Processing details

| Required detail | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Subject matter | Provision, support, security and administration of the Leo HR cloud software service |
| Duration | The subscription/call-off term plus the documented return, deletion and legally required retention period |
| Nature and purpose | Storing, organising, retrieving, displaying, transmitting and otherwise processing workforce data to provide customer-selected HR workflows |
| Data subjects | Customer users, employees, workers, applicants, former personnel, referees, emergency contacts and other individuals recorded by the Customer |
| Personal data | Identity and contact details; employment, contractual, attendance, performance, conduct, recruitment, training, qualification, compliance and audit records; documents and correspondence uploaded or generated through the service |

## 3. Processor obligations

Leo will, in relation to Customer personal data:

1. process it only on the Customer's documented instructions, including for international transfers, unless UK law requires otherwise; where legally permitted, Leo will tell the Customer before carrying out legally required processing;
2. ensure people authorised to process it are subject to confidentiality obligations;
3. implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures proportionate to the risk;
4. engage subprocessors only under the arrangements in section 4;
5. taking account of the nature of the processing, assist the Customer through appropriate technical and organisational measures with data-subject rights requests;
6. assist the Customer, taking account of the processing and information available to Leo, with security, breach, impact-assessment and prior-consultation obligations;
7. at the Customer's choice and subject to applicable law, return or delete Customer personal data at the end of the services in accordance with the agreed exit schedule;
8. make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with these processor obligations and allow proportionate audits or inspections under agreed security, confidentiality, timing and cost controls; and
9. inform the Customer if, in Leo's opinion, an instruction infringes applicable data-protection law.

The current procurement security evidence pack describes these measures without implying unheld certifications or untested guarantees.

## 6. Personal-data breaches

Leo will notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting Customer personal data and will provide available information reasonably needed for the Customer to assess and meet its notification duties. Notification is not an admission of fault or liability.

## 10. Liability and precedence

Liability is governed by the main agreement and any mandatory framework terms. If this DPA conflicts with the main agreement on the protection of Customer personal data, this DPA prevails to that extent, subject to mandatory framework terms.

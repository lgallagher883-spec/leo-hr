# Leo HR — Environmental Position

**Supplier:** LEO HR LTD  
**Status:** Proportionate SME working statement  
**Last reviewed:** 12 September 2026

## Position

Leo HR is a cloud software service and does not manufacture or distribute physical products as part of its ordinary subscription. Its direct environmental impacts are expected to arise principally through cloud services, business equipment, travel, purchased services and any office use.

Leo will:

- prefer digital delivery and documentation where suitable;
- avoid unnecessary printing, travel and physical distribution;
- use equipment responsibly and dispose of electronic equipment through appropriate routes;
- consider environmental credentials when selecting material suppliers where proportionate;
- monitor relevant business travel and material energy/cloud information as the business grows;
- review this position annually and before making a contract-specific carbon commitment.

## Claims boundary

Leo does not currently claim net zero, carbon neutrality, independently verified emissions figures or a completed Carbon Reduction Plan. If a procurement requires a formal plan or emissions baseline, Leo will assess the applicable requirement and prepare evidence using the required methodology before making a claim.

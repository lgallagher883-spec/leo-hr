# Leo HR — G-Cloud Pricing (Draft)

**Supplier:** LEO HR LTD  
**Service:** Leo HR  
**Last reviewed:** 12 September 2026

## Proposed public-sector subscription tiers

| Organisation capacity | Monthly equivalent | Annual subscription |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Up to 50 employees | £250 | £3,000 |
| Up to 150 employees | £500 | £6,000 |
| Up to 250 employees | £750 | £9,000 |
| Up to 500 employees | £1,250 | £15,000 |
| Up to 1,000 employees | £2,000 | £24,000 |
| More than 1,000 employees | Priced for the stated requirement | Published quotation / call-off |

These are recommended working prices for G-Cloud and other public-sector procurement. They require commercial approval before publication. The normal SME website prices remain separate and are not the proposed public-sector tariff.

## Included service

The subscription provides access to the Leo HR platform up to the stated active-employee capacity. The price reflects the higher assurance, procurement, accessibility, contract-management and support burden associated with public-sector supply as well as expected platform and AI usage. Final submission wording should list any tier-specific feature restrictions only if they exist in the deployed entitlement model.

## Invoicing and VAT

- Public-sector subscriptions will be invoiced separately against a valid purchase order and paid by bank transfer; Stripe/card payment is not required.
- Recommended payment term: 30 days from a valid invoice.
- Recommended billing: annually in advance, with quarterly invoicing available where agreed in the call-off.
- LEO HR LTD is not currently VAT-registered, so VAT is not currently charged. If its VAT status changes, VAT will be applied where legally required at the prevailing rate.

## Recommended commercial protections

- Minimum initial term: 12 months.
- Capacity is based on active employee records, with the next band applying when the contracted limit is exceeded.
- Ordinary onboarding, standard employee-data import and administrator orientation should be included.
- Bespoke migration, integrations, configuration, development or onsite work should be separately scoped and priced.
- Normal AI-assisted use should be included, subject to acceptable-use and service-protection controls. Materially exceptional or automated bulk consumption must be separately agreed.
- Pricing for more than 1,000 employees should account for expected administrators, managers, AI usage, storage, migration and support rather than employee count alone.

## Items to confirm before publication

- approval of the proposed public-sector prices above;
- charges for bespoke migration, additional training, premium support or requested development;
- the pricing formula and ceiling for organisations above 250 employees;
- any volume, education, charity or public-sector discount;
- exit/export assistance included in the subscription and any chargeable additional work.

## Pricing principles

- State all mandatory costs needed to use the service.
- Separate optional professional services from the cloud-software subscription.
- Do not make bespoke consultancy a hidden requirement for ordinary use.
- Apply the published price consistently through the call-off unless the framework permits and records a valid discount.
- Keep this document consistent with the live application, order form and contract terms.

## Free trial

The standard commercial service currently offers a seven-day free trial. For public-sector procurement, a controlled demonstration or time-limited trial using synthetic data is preferable. Any live-data trial must be covered by appropriate contract and data-processing terms.

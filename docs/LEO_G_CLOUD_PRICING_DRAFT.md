# Leo HR — G-Cloud Pricing (Draft)

**Supplier:** LEO HR LTD  
**Service:** Leo HR  
**Last reviewed:** 12 September 2026

## Current standard subscription tiers

| Organisation capacity | Current standard price | Billing basis |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Up to 50 employees | £75 per month | Organisation subscription |
| Up to 150 employees | £125 per month | Organisation subscription |
| Up to 250 employees | £175 per month | Organisation subscription |
| More than 250 employees | Tailored | Subject to agreed requirements |

## Included service

The standard subscription provides access to the Leo HR platform up to the stated active-employee capacity. Final submission wording should list any tier-specific feature restrictions only if they exist in the deployed entitlement model.

## Items to confirm before publication

- whether the prices above will be used for G-Cloud buyers;
- whether prices are exclusive or inclusive of VAT;
- monthly versus annual invoicing and payment terms;
- minimum call-off term;
- whether standard onboarding, data import and administrator orientation are included;
- charges for bespoke migration, additional training, premium support or requested development;
- the pricing formula and ceiling for organisations above 250 employees;
- any volume, education, charity or public-sector discount;
- exit/export assistance included in the subscription and any chargeable additional work.

## Pricing principles

- State all mandatory costs needed to use the service.
- Separate optional professional services from the cloud-software subscription.
- Do not make bespoke consultancy a hidden requirement for ordinary use.
- Apply the published price consistently through the call-off unless the framework permits and records a valid discount.
- Keep this document consistent with the live application, order form and contract terms.

## Free trial

The standard commercial service currently offers a seven-day free trial. A G-Cloud listing should only advertise this if the same offer will be available to public-sector buyers on clearly stated terms.

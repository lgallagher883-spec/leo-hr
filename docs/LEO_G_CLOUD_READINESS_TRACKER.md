Leo will answer procurement questions from deployed or documentary evidence. The submission must not claim ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials, SOC 2, penetration-test certification, guaranteed uptime, tested RTO/RPO or universal UK-only data residency unless current evidence supports the claim.

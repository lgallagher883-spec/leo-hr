# Leo HR — Modern Slavery and Human Trafficking Position

**Supplier:** LEO HR LTD  
**Status:** Voluntary proportionate supplier statement  
**Last reviewed:** 12 September 2026

LEO HR LTD is a small UK software business and is not currently understood to meet the turnover threshold that creates the statutory annual statement duty under section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015. Leo nevertheless opposes slavery, forced labour, servitude and human trafficking.

Leo will take proportionate steps to:

- use reputable suppliers and review material new suppliers where labour or supply-chain risk is relevant;
- avoid knowingly contracting with organisations involved in exploitation;
- raise and investigate credible concerns connected with its operations or supply chain;
- cooperate with lawful customer due-diligence requirements;
- review this position if Leo's size, activities, suppliers or legal obligations change.

No statement should imply that Leo has conducted a global supply-chain audit or is subject to the statutory publication duty unless that becomes true.

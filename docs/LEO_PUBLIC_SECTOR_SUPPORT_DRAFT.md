# Leo HR — Public-Sector Support and Service Management (Draft)

**Status:** Recommended position; approval required before contractual use  
**Last reviewed:** 12 September 2026

## Standard support

- Support hours: 09:00–17:00 UK time, Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in England.
- Channels: Help Scout/email and the in-product support route.
- Service notices: supplied through appropriate customer contact channels for material incidents or planned maintenance.
- Resolution times depend on diagnosis, third-party providers and the nature of the defect; acknowledgement targets are not guaranteed resolution times.

## Proposed acknowledgement targets

| Priority | Description | Target acknowledgement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 Critical | Confirmed serious data exposure/loss, broken tenant isolation, or widespread inability to access the service | Within 2 support hours |
| P2 High | A core function unavailable to multiple users with no reasonable workaround | Within 4 support hours |
| P3 Medium | A material but bounded fault where a reasonable workaround exists | Within 1 business day |
| P4 Low | Minor functional, content, usability or cosmetic issue | Within 2 business days |

## Outside support hours

No staffed 24/7 helpdesk is included in the standard service. Customers must not be told that Leo provides round-the-clock human support unless a separately resourced service is agreed. Reports received outside support hours are measured from the next support period.

## Availability

Leo uses managed cloud infrastructure and monitors production operation through its providers and application workflow. No contractual uptime percentage is offered until service history and monitoring evidence support it. Buyer-specific availability commitments must be separately assessed and priced.

## Planned maintenance and change

Routine application changes use tests and preview deployments before controlled production promotion. Where planned work is reasonably expected to cause material disruption, Leo will aim to give advance notice through the nominated customer contact. Emergency security or recovery changes may be made without advance notice where delay would increase risk.

## Escalation

Customers should provide the affected function, time, users, screenshots/error text and business impact. P1/P2 reports are escalated to the service owner. Material personal-data incidents follow the incident and DPA processes.

## Exclusions

Targets may be affected by matters outside Leo's reasonable control, customer systems, unsupported browsers, customer configuration, third-party integrations or provider incidents. This does not remove obligations that apply under the contract or law.

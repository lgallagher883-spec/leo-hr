# Leo HR — Social Value and Responsible Business Position

**Supplier:** LEO HR LTD  
**Last reviewed:** 12 September 2026

## Purpose

Leo HR is a small UK software supplier. Its social-value commitments should be proportionate, relevant to each contract and measurable. Leo will not promise programmes it lacks the resources to deliver.

## Proposed commitments

### Better employment practice

Support public-sector customers and their supply chains in maintaining clearer, fairer and more consistent employment processes through accessible HR workflows, practical resources and auditable records.

### SME and local economic participation

Where proportionate to delivery, consider UK SMEs and local suppliers for professional and operational services. Record supplier location and SME status for relevant contract expenditure.

### Inclusive and accessible service

Work towards WCAG 2.2 AA, test representative employee and employer journeys, publish known limitations and prioritise barriers affecting authentication, navigation and essential employment tasks.

### Responsible AI

Maintain human responsibility for employment decisions, permission-controlled access, traceable workflow boundaries and clear limitations. AI will support professional judgement rather than autonomously determine employment outcomes.

### Skills and participation

As the business grows, consider proportionate work-experience, early-career or small-business learning opportunities connected to software, responsible AI, HR administration or digital operations.

## Measurement

For each public contract, select only relevant measures, such as:

- number of customer administrators trained;
- accessibility issues identified and remediated;
- percentage/value of relevant spend with UK SMEs;
- responsible-AI or fair-process learning materials supplied;
- work-experience or early-career hours delivered;
- improvements arising from customer accessibility feedback.

Targets must be agreed per call-off and reported using evidence rather than estimates.
